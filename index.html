About SCOREFREE

Free bank exam practice: no login, no fees

SCOREFREE is a free practice website for bank exam aspirants. You can take IBPS Clerk Mains and SBI Clerk Mains sectional tests in Reasoning Ability, Quantitative Aptitude, English Language and General/Financial Awareness, and practise IBPS PO and SBI PO descriptive writing (essay, comprehension, email, situation analysis, report and précis).

The test window works like the real exam: one question at a time, a live countdown timer for each section, a question palette, Mark for Review, and negative marking of one-quarter mark for each wrong answer. Section question counts, marks and timings follow the latest official exam patterns. Please always confirm them in the official IBPS and SBI notifications.

How many tests are there?

Each exam has 10 sectional tests in every subject. The test windows are ready, and we are adding questions test by test, so some tests are still being prepared. New tests are added regularly. Come back often or bookmark this page.

Frequently asked questions

Is SCOREFREE really free? Yes. There is no sign-up, login or payment.

Are these the real exam questions? No. Practice questions are original. Any question that was reported from a previous exam is labelled Reported PYQ.

Is the score an official score? No. It is a practice score to help you track accuracy. SCOREFREE is not affiliated with IBPS or SBI.

Which exams will be added next? More Clerk Mains tests first. Tell us what you need using the suggestion link at the bottom of the page.
