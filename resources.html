Free resource directory

Clerk Mains papers, all in one place.

A curated starting point for free, publicly accessible IBPS Clerk Mains and SBI Clerk Mains memory-based papers, recalled questions, videos and study material. Open the original source to view or download each resource.

Important: these are memory-based/reconstructed materials, not official exam papers. Some websites mix free links with paid tests, so check each item before downloading. SCOREFREE links to third-party resources and does not claim ownership of them. Free-to-access does not automatically mean permission to republish a complete PDF; this page keeps the material linked to its original publisher.
